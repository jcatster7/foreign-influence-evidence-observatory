# What the public matched-feed evidence jointly says

## Bottom line

Two independent public studies now support a narrow but meaningful conclusion: X's ranked For You feed did not simply mirror what users chose to follow. It systematically changed the composition of exposure in both the 2023 and 2024 samples. The studies do not measure the same construct, so their coefficients must not be pooled.

In the July-September 2023 data, the participant-equal conservative-relative For You uplift was **2.161 percentage points**, with simultaneous 95% interval **[0.095, 4.226]**. The participant distribution was nearly split, so this was an average compositional asymmetry rather than a uniform experience.

In the September-October 2024 data, followed-account inventories aligned with participants' stated values, with mean Spearman correlation **0.302 [0.284, 0.320]**. The values selected for amplification were instead negatively aligned, **-0.151 [-0.169, -0.132]**. Misalignment was larger for Democrats, **-0.229 [-0.251, -0.207]**, than Republicans, **-0.041 [-0.067, -0.015]**.

## 2024 value-selection pattern

| Schwartz value cluster | Amplification coefficient | Approximate 95% Wald CI |
|---|---:|---:|
| Conservation | +0.133 | [+0.119, +0.147] |
| Openness to Change | +0.034 | [+0.024, +0.045] |
| Self-Enhancement | -0.036 | [-0.050, -0.023] |
| Self-Transcendence | -0.054 | [-0.063, -0.046] |

The 2024 feed favored Conservation and Openness to Change while demoting Self-Enhancement and Self-Transcendence. These psychological-value categories are not synonyms for conservative and liberal ideology; the partisan result is the stronger misalignment for Democrats, not a relabeling of Conservation as Republican content.

## What replicates across the studies

- **Selection changes exposure:** For You differed systematically from the followed-account baseline in both periods.
- **An average masks heterogeneity:** the 2023 ideological shift was nearly evenly split across participants; the 2024 value alignment differed sharply by party.
- **Engagement is an incomplete preference proxy:** the 2024 evidence shows that selected content can be negatively aligned with explicit user values even when followed accounts are aligned.
- **A single left-right score is insufficient:** source type, psychological values, and user-level variation all change the interpretation.

## What does not replicate—or has not been tested

The studies do not establish a common numeric rightward effect across 2023 and 2024. One measures source-account ideology in percentage points; the other measures value-expression selection and rank correlation. Neither identifies platform intent, persuasion, a population-wide effect, or the behavior of X's September 2026 production algorithm. Public privacy protections also prevent naming the accounts or posts responsible.

## Decision for the research program

Treat the current evidence as a two-period historical baseline. The strongest defensible claim is that For You altered political/value exposure relative to users' chosen networks, with a modest average conservative-relative shift in 2023 and value misalignment—especially for Democrats—in 2024. A present-day claim still requires new matched feed rows or a newly released 2025-2026 dataset.
