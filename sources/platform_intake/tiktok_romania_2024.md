The page also reports broad fake-like, spam, and impersonation enforcement. Those totals have different units and are not evidence that the listed covert networks were automated, exposed to audiences, recommended, or persuasive. TikTok's statement about the Turkey-origin network is especially useful for the O/P boundary: a platform may locate network operations while explicitly lacking the director's identity and location. This intake does not turn the page into account-level ground truth.
