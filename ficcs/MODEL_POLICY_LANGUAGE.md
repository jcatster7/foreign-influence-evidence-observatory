# FICCS model policy language

Candidate language for platforms, public agencies, researchers, and newsrooms. Bracketed fields are mandatory substitutions.

## Public claim

> We assess that [subject] at the [unit] level during [period] supports the following proposition(s): [construct codes and plain-language names]. This assessment relies on [evidence stage] evidence from [authority and source], located at [locator]. It does not establish [missing constructs or links]. Confidence is [high/moderate/low] because [reason]. The machine-readable disclosure and correction history are available at [URL].

## Platform attribution

> [Platform] attributes [campaign or operation] to [actor] within the scope of [accounts/content/period]. This is a platform-disclosure basis for P. It does not by itself establish the origin, automation, coordination, deception, delivery, recommendation, or impact of every included account or item. The presently unresolved constructs are [list].

## Proxy-only signal

> We observed [displayed location/bot score/posting pattern/interaction pattern]. This is a proxy for [construct], not direct verification. The decision is `unknown` unless additional case-specific evidence satisfies the FICCS minimum-evidence rule.

## Exposure and recommendation

> The data measure [potential exposure/observed delivery/engagement]. They [do/do not] directly observe feed delivery. A recommendation increment is supported only if the analysis includes [explicit eligible, chronological, or followed baseline] for the same unit, period, and outcome. Audience impact remains [decision] under [design].

## Institutional action

> The attached action is [category] and is [recommended/documented as taken/both]. Its descriptive severity is [low/moderate/high]. The action relies on [supported constructs]. The unresolved links that could change the action are [list]. Review or correction requests may be submitted through [route].

## Correction

> This record supersedes [record ID]. We changed [fields] because [new evidence or correction]. The earlier record remains available at [version-history URL]. No unchanged construct has been silently upgraded.
