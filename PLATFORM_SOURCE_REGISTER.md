## Cross-platform intake rules

1. Preserve the platform's exact attribution phrase and source date; do not convert “linked to,” “believed,” “investigated,” or “suspicious” into verified operator nationality.
2. Treat a report, an indicator list, an account archive, and an audience exposure dataset as different source types. Their denominators and rights differ.
3. Split by underlying campaign/dataset before synthesis. The same operation may appear in several platform reports and papers; link those records without counting them as independent confirmations.
4. Mark E, R, and I `unknown` unless delivery, an explicit feed/ranking comparison, or an audience outcome is measured under the codebook. A platform's removal or engagement count alone does not satisfy those constructs.
5. This register is a fixed starting index for later registered searches. It does not claim that every campaign or report has been found.
