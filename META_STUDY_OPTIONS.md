# Meta-study options from the current evidence map

Status: decision memo from pre-registration scoping, 2026-09-20 UTC. The search is incomplete and no final study count or pooled effect is available. These options are research designs, not findings. All would keep external spend at $0 by default and below the $20 cap; independent review time is the main resource constraint.

| Option | Core question and publishable result | Quantitative synthesis gate | Current feasibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Evidence-chain validity map — recommended** | Across platforms, which of verified operator origin (O), campaign attribution (P), automation (A), coordination (C), deception (D), observed feed delivery (E), recommendation increment (R), and audience impact (I) are actually measured? Publish a dataset-level node/edge map and a claim-boundary benchmark with explicit unknowns. | Descriptive counts after complete deduplicated screening and two-coder extraction. Pool an effect only for at least three independent datasets sharing construct, denominator, comparison, and uncertainty. | The [protocol](PREREGISTRATION_DRAFT.md), [codebook](EXTRACTION_CODEBOOK.md), [source register](PLATFORM_SOURCE_REGISTER.md), and [benchmark](BENCHMARK_SPEC.md) already implement this direction. The main work remaining is OSF registration, full search and screening, and independent coding. |
| **2. False-positive meta-analysis for bot and operation detectors** | At fixed, documented thresholds, how often do detectors misclassify independently verified human or unrelated accounts? Stratify by platform, language, time, detector version, and negative-label provenance. Report confusion matrices and abstention coverage. | At least three independent datasets with genuine verified negatives and compatible threshold/metric definitions; use binomial counts and denominators, not scores or unverified controls. | [Rauchfleisch and Kaiser](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0241045) publish a historical 2019 Botometer v3 validation with [public data and code](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/XVCKRS). Their official politician accounts are proxies for fully human operation, and the authors could not individually verify every label. Reanalysis can supply a historical reference-group error rate if score fields and thresholds are confirmed, but it cannot establish a 2026 population rate. [Saeed et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.18098) need full-text negative-sampling review; the restricted controls in [Seçkin et al.](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICWSM/article/view/35958) are not verified negatives. A pooled current-world false-positive rate is not yet defensible. |
| **3. Foreign-operation audience-outcome synthesis** | What associations or causal effects have been measured between contact with an attributed operation and changes in attitudes or behavior? Publish comparable effect estimates separately by exposure measure and design. | Pool only if at least three independent studies use the same exposure/contact definition, outcome scale, population, time window, and recoverable uncertainty. Separate randomized exposure from observational contact. | [Bail et al.](https://sites.duke.edu/hillygus/files/2020/02/2020.PNAS_.Bailetal.pdf) measured 2017 IRA interactions and later outcomes; [Eady et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9829855/) reconstructed potential 2016 exposure and survey outcomes. Their periods, exposure measures, samples, and estimands differ. A structured synthesis is currently safer than a pooled “impact” number. |
| **4. Attribution-to-recommendation linkage study** | For the same campaign accounts, can platform attribution be linked to observed feed delivery and a ranking counterfactual? A negative result would precisely locate the missing data link; a positive result would estimate the increment. | Requires a common account/campaign identifier, audience feed observations, eligible-content baseline, and uncertainty for at least three independent datasets before meta-analysis. | [Huszár et al.](https://www.pnas.org/doi/full/10.1073/pnas.2025334119) have a randomized ranking comparison for political source groups, without foreign-operation account labels. The public local matched-feed studies have aggregate or anonymized account data. [Eady et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9829855/) have attributed accounts and potential exposure but not observed delivery or ranking comparison. The joint estimand is currently unmeasured in the inspected material. |

## Recommendation

Launch **Option 1** as the observatory's main preregistered meta-study. It can produce a rigorous, useful result even when a causal link or pooled effect is absent: exactly which claim transitions are measured, which rely on proxies, and where independent data are missing. Treat **Option 2** as the first conditional numerical meta-analysis after verified-negative provenance and compatible thresholds are established. Report Options 3 and 4 as separate structured syntheses or gap analyses until their pooling gates pass. The absence of eligible estimates is an outcome of the review, not permission to substitute engagement, account suspension, or displayed country for the intended measures.

## Decision and resource gates

- Do not compute a pooled effect from the current seed map. It contains leads and overlapping analyses, not adjudicated independent datasets.
- Preregister the chosen estimand, outcome scale, study-independence rule, model, and sensitivity analyses before fitting any meta-analysis. The current registration candidate specifies the first-stage eligibility and minimum pooling threshold; a numerical model needs a later registered amendment if the gate passes.
- Two independent coders are required for final full-text inclusion and construct labels. The current OpenAlex scoping queue has 7,986 unscreened records before the remaining cursor and supplemental sources, so reviewer labor is substantial despite a $0 cash plan.
- Use [PRISMA 2020](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020) for the search flow and [SWiM](https://www.bmj.com/content/368/bmj.l6890) for synthesis without a defensible pool. Report dataset overlap and uncertainty explicitly.
