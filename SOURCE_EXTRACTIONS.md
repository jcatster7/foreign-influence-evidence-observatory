# Provisional source extractions

Version 0.1, 2026-09-19. These are auditable scoping extractions, not final review adjudications. Claim codes: O account/operator origin, P campaign attribution, A automation, C coordination, D deception, E observed exposure, R ranking increment, I audience impact. A code listed as measured does not imply a positive effect. Full systematic screening, independent coding, and risk-of-bias assessment are pending.

## Cross-source synthesis at this stage

The inspected material supplies separate pieces of the causal chain: attributed operations with limited account-level verification; potential exposure to campaign-attributed accounts; randomized ranking effects for political sources without foreign-operation labels; and local matched-feed source/value selection without identifiable foreign accounts. No inspected dataset jointly establishes verified individual foreign origin, automation, covert coordination, ranking increment, and audience impact for the same accounts. This is a provisional statement about the sources above, not a conclusion from a completed systematic search.
